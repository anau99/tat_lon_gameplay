📜 How to play

  1. Move with W/A/S/D or arrow keys. As Thrower, choose a spot behind the line.
  2. Aim, adjust power, then press Space or THROW!. You can move while the slipper flies.
  3. Can stays upright: The Thrower may retrieve the slipper. The Guard must tag the Thrower holding it outside the safe area to swap roles.
  4. Can falls: The Thrower may retrieve the slipper. The Guard must return the fallen can to the circle before tagging anyone.
  5. After resetting the can, the Guard can tag the Thrower only while the Thrower holds the slipper outside the safe area. Touching a slipper on the ground never swaps roles.
  6. If the Thrower returns behind the safe line with the slipper, they are safe and may throw again.
  7. On a role swap, you play the other role and the AI takes over your previous role. The next round starts automatically.
On mobile, use the directional pad in the field. As Guard, tag the Thrower carrying the slipper; if the can falls, restore it first.
